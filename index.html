A few useful details.

Do I need an account?

No account is needed to write locally. Open the notebook and start. Optional encrypted sync lets you carry your entries across devices.

Can I take my notes with me?

Export your entries as JSON for backup or import them into another WorkLedger installation. Individual notes can be downloaded as Markdown. You can archive and restore entries, or delete them.

What happens when I am offline?

The installed app supports offline writing. Sync and remote AI need a connection. Back up your local entries before clearing browser data.

Is WorkLedger open source?

Yes. The app is MIT licensed. Read the code, contribute, or host your own copy.
